# Whetstone data: sources, licences and notices

Every file under `current/<game>/` and `history/` is produced by the Whetstone pipeline from the sources below.
Each game directory's `_files.json` gives every file's custody class: A (open, or our own derivation),
A-SA (share-alike, kept in separate files) and A-NC-SA (non-commercial share-alike). Please keep the credits
below when you reuse the data.
